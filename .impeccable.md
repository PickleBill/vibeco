## Design Context

### Users
People exploring a business idea, researching a company or topic, testing an
initiative, or working through a decision. Bill also uses the same workbench for
his own research and interview preparation. Interviewers should see commercial
judgment expressed through a working product, with evidence and limits visible.

Users speak in outcomes. Start with their question; let them discover the deeper
capabilities through the work. Preserve the build simulator and advanced tools.

### Brand Personality
Curious, commercially grounded, expressive, and candid.
A thoughtful working partner who can challenge an assumption and help make the
next step concrete. No inflated delivery claims or implied customer evidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
The September 2026 approved revamp supersedes the former cinematic dark-mode
preference: warm cream paper, deep teal ink, restrained green and yellow notes.
Use the existing Sora and Albert Sans families, generous reading space, editorial
composition, and visual demonstrations of actual question-to-output behavior.

Avoid neural orbs, glass panels, gradients as decoration, repetitive card grids,
stock AI imagery, and unsupported testimonials. Advanced reports may retain their
established controls while improving readability and progressive disclosure.

### Design Principles
1. **Make the first useful action obvious.** One question, four optional lenses.
2. **Show the work.** Let visitors inspect an instant, clearly labeled example.
3. **Earn complexity.** Lead with a recommendation and reveal analytical depth.
4. **Distinguish evidence from interpretation.** Synthetic perspectives are not
   interviews; model agreement is not verification.
5. **Keep the person in charge.** No automatic model runs, private work by default,
   truthful save states, clear alternatives when something fails.
6. **Make it comfortable to use.** Responsive layouts, visible keyboard focus,
   44px primary targets, descriptive labels, and respect for reduced motion.

### Public Navigation
Workbench · Examples · About Bill. Saved work lives in the account menu; owner
administration appears only for authorized users. Brick is the reciprocal career
site; VibeCo is the working lab. Preserve legacy routes and homepage anchors.
